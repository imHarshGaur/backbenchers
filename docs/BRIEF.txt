- Theme: Pure 90s/2000s Indian school-bench nostalgia combined with high-stakes competitive tension.

THE 3 CORE GAMES (ALL IN ONE APK):

GAME 1: PEN ROCKET (LINE-SLICING BATTLE ROYALE)
- Mechanic: Players hold and drag back on their vertical pen tip on a ruled notebook page to set angle and flick power, then release. The pen tip slides across the paper, leaving a permanent colored ink line behind it.
- Win Condition: If your sliding pen tip intersects/slices through an opponent's existing ink line, their line shatters and they are eliminated (or lose a life in 4–6 player Royale). Last pen standing wins the pot.
- High-Stakes Twists:
  - Shrinking "Red Margin" danger zone every 3 turns that eliminates pens outside the border.
  - Interactive Desk Hazards: Coffee cup rings (deflect trajectory), Compass Holes (teleport ink trail across the page), and Whitener Drops (grants a 1-hit shield).
  - Pen Classes: Ballpoint (balanced), Gel Pen (long slide, slight curve), Fountain Pen (short slide, requires 2 cuts to break).

GAME 2: DOTS & BOXES (CHAUKADI BLITZ)
- Mechanic: Classic grid of dots on graph paper (4x4 for fast 1v1, 6x6 for 3–4 players). Players tap between two adjacent dots to draw a pen stroke. Completing the 4th side of a square claims the box with the player's stamped initial and awards a bonus turn.
- High-Stakes Twists:
  - "Speed Blitz" 6-second turn timer (running out of time auto-draws a random line).
  - "Golden Star Boxes" (2x points) and "Ink Bomb Dots" (completing a box next to a bomb blasts open neighboring lines).

GAME 3: RAJA MANTRI CHOR SIPAHI (RMCS - 4 TO 6 PLAYERS)
- Mechanic: Folded paper chits drop onto the wooden desk and shuffle with physics. Each player picks/receives a secret chit.
- Roles & Points:
  - 4-Player Mode: Raja (1000 pts), Mantri (800 pts), Sipahi (500 pts), Chor (0 pts).
  - 5-Player Expansion: Adds Senapati (650 pts).
  - 6-Player Expansion: Adds Daroga (400 pts) & Joker/Bhatija (250 pts).
- Gameplay Flow:
  1. Raja reveals their chit ("Mera Mantri Kaun?").
  2. Mantri reveals themselves and has 15 seconds to interrogate/read the table and tap the player they suspect is the Chor.
  3. High-Stakes Twist: Once per 5-round match, the Chor can trigger a "Chit Swap" bluff card before the Mantri guesses, or players can place a side-bet ("Chalk Bet") on whether the Mantri will catch the Chor.
  4. If Mantri guesses right, points stay as drawn. If wrong, Chor steals Mantri's 800 points!
  5. Multi-round leaderboard (5 or 7 rounds) crowns the "Class Topper".
